# Gekke achtergronden

Gemaakt met de ingebouwde imagegen-tool. Het PNG-bestand bevat vier gelijke panelen: theepotten linksboven, snoepland rechtsboven, ruimte linksonder en kwallen rechtsonder. De renderer gebruikt de panelen afzonderlijk.

## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: background image atlas for the child-friendly Bravios platform game, four equal rectangular panels in an exact 2 by 2 grid, no borders, no gutters, no text. Each panel is a complete wide 3:2 landscape, total canvas 3:2 aspect ratio. Painterly polished whimsical storybook illustration, muted dreamy colors so foreground game platforms remain easy to see. Top left: surreal lavender sky with floating teapot islands pouring waterfalls, upside-down trees and distant tiny planets. Top right: peach and mint dreamscape of gigantic mushrooms, striped candy mountains and fish swimming through clouds. Bottom left: deep blue outer space with sleepy crescent moons, floating clocks, spiral galaxies and tiny whale-shaped planets. Bottom right: turquoise dream sea with floating jellyfish balloons, crooked coral towers and impossible looping pipes in the distance. Atmospheric layered depth, detailed scenery throughout each panel, no solid playable floor, no game characters, no interface, no lettering. These are intentionally strange and funny BACKGROUND environments, soft contrast.
