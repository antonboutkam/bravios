# Bravios: aansluitende grond en baksteen

Gemaakt met de ingebouwde image_gen-tool, geen CLI. Referenties: assets/game1.png en assets/game3.png. Bronvellen: ground-variants.png en brick-variants.png. De game bereidt de gedeelde randen in terrain.mjs voor.

## Grond

Asset type: ONE production sprite atlas, 5 columns x 4 rows = exactly 20 square terrain texture cells. Primary request: 20 compatible variations of orange-brown soil for the Bravios 2D platform game, matching the hand-painted cheerful cartoon shading and rock shapes of the ground tiles in the reference. Reference image is ONLY a style and material reference; do not reproduce its layout. Composition: exact regular full-bleed 5 by 4 grid filling the ENTIRE image, absolutely zero gutters or margins, every cell square, identical cell sizes. Each cell is a flat front-facing earth cross-section, not a freestanding object. No grass at the top because the game adds grass along exposed edges. All cells share the same medium warm ochre base color at their edges, and have varied subtle interior details: differently shaped slate gray pebbles, little orange strata, root threads, rounded stones, compact dirt grains; keep major details in the central 75% of each cell. Vary placement and shapes appreciably across all 20 cells but retain the exact same palette and light direction. Lighting: gentle top-left illustrated highlights, flat side-view game art. Constraints: every square fully opaque soil, no perspective, no block outline, no framing, no grass, no sky, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no white gaps, no checkerboard. This is a texture atlas with 20 distinct soil patches, not a presentation sheet. Target 5:4 image aspect ratio with precisely aligned equal square cells.

## Baksteen

Asset type: ONE production sprite atlas, 5 columns x 4 rows = exactly 20 square masonry texture cells. Primary request: 20 compatible variations of warm terracotta brick wall for Bravios, a cheerful polished cartoon 2D platform game. The reference image is ONLY a reference for painted cartoon rendering, shading, and dark outlines; replace the wooden crate material with real terracotta BRICKS. Composition: exact regular full-bleed 5 by 4 grid filling the ENTIRE image, absolutely zero gutters or margins, every cell square and identical size. Each cell shows flat front-facing red-orange clay masonry wall with TWO horizontal courses of bricks in running bond, upper course has vertical seam at the middle, lower course seams at left/right edges. All cells have precisely identical mortar-line positions and common reddish-brown base tone at edges so cells can be tiled in any order. Horizontal mortar at exactly 0%, 50% and 100% tile height. Varied tasteful details within bricks: tiny chips, hairline cracks, rounded highlights, subtly darker clay speckles, small moss patches on a few cells, clean/worn variations. Major details remain in inner 75% of each square. Cartoon hand painted finish in the reference style, light from top left, warm red-orange clay with dark brown thin mortar. Constraints: flat wall texture all the way to every edge, no standalone box/block silhouettes, no perspective, no wooden crates, no studs, no grass plinths, no framing, no labels, no text, no numbers, no white gaps, no checkerboard. Texture atlas with twenty variations, not a display sheet. Target 5:4 image aspect ratio with precisely aligned equal square cells.
