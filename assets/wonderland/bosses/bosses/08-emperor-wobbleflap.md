# 08 — Emperor Wobbleflap the Everything-Eater

**Realm:** The Edge of Wonderland  
**Role:** Final collaborative boss; revisits the seven earlier skills.  
**Palette:** Midnight indigo / deep plum / seven rainbow crown gems.  
**Silhouette:** Huge three-headed, six-armed creature with starry belly and a floating seven-gem crown.

## Personality and entrance
Wobbleflap has swallowed the rules of Wonderland. Gravity, direction, music and even breakfast now behave strangely. “I ATE THE RULEBOOK! IT WAS DELICIOUS!”

## Arena
A shifting patchwork island suspended over a dreamy starry void. Eight themed sections echo the seven earlier realms plus a central rainbow dais. Wobbling tiles, candy hooks, mirror platforms and clock gears are reused gently.

## Attacks and three-phase encounter
1. **The First Three Gems:** Wobbleflap summons purple jelly waves, waffle syrup trails and colorful sock tornadoes. Players use Slime Bounce, Syrup Swing and Sock Cyclone to hit three exposed crown gems.
2. **The Middle Two Gems:** His clock-head slows the arena; then his thunder-head starts a dance pattern. Time Pause and Beat Dash let players reach two more gems.
3. **The Last Two Gems:** The walking-castle section rises and the mirror section creates false copies. Castle Stomp and Mirror Twin reveal and activate the final pair of gems.

## Puzzle weakness
The crown holds seven magical gems, one for each earlier boss. Boaz and Ravi cooperate at the final central platform: one keeps a giant pressure plate active while the other reaches the crown. In solo mode, the Mirror Twin ability operates the second plate. A failed attempt returns the player safely to the preceding platform.

## Win moment and reward
The crown dissolves into rainbow confetti. The enormous emperor shrinks into a tiny friendly white bunny, and Wonderland's rules return to normal—except for the fun ones. Reward: **Wonderland Restored** and a celebratory rainbow path through every region.

**Combat readability:** Never require all seven abilities in a single time-critical sequence. Show the next gemstone and relevant power icon before each challenge.

**Suggested SFX:** An orchestral medley of seven boss themes ending with a tiny bunny squeak.
