# 07 — Princess Mirrorphantom

**Realm:** The Mirror Palace  
**Role:** An observation puzzle about reflections and false copies.  
**Palette:** Chrome silver / soft lavender / cobalt blue.  
**Silhouette:** Giant elegant chameleon with three faces, faceted mirror wings and a long coiled tail.

## Personality and entrance
The princess adores looking at herself, but her three faces can never agree on which side looks best. “MIRROR, MIRROR… WAIT, WHICH ONE OF YOU IS ME?”

## Arena
A palace floor of bright mirror tiles and shifting reflections, interrupted by safe matte islands. A few large tiles gently swing to change the angle of reflected light.

## Moves and phases
1. **Mirror Multiplication:** Creates several shimmering clones that follow her movements a fraction of a second late.
2. **Invisible Spin:** She disappears, but her shadow and tiny red nose stay visible.
3. **Reflection Shuffle (phase 2):** Mirror panels rotate and the clones swap apparent positions.

## Puzzle weakness
The real princess is the only version whose reflected face has a red nose. Follow the correct reflection, then touch the glowing blue mirror button beside her; her clones pop into bubbles. Repeat for three rounds.

## Win moment and reward
She sneezes, every mirror shows a ridiculous facial expression, and she gives **Mirror Twin**: a temporary friendly clone that can stand on a second pressure plate.

**Combat readability:** Show a small red-nose tutorial image before the first round. Keep reflections legible on low-end displays; avoid aggressive flashing.

**Suggested SFX:** Glassy musical chimes, rubber squeak, echoing giggle.
