# 04 — Tick-Tock the Time Eater

**Realm:** The Backwards Clockwork  
**Role:** Introduces temporary time tricks and altered movement, with clear warnings.  
**Palette:** Antique brass / bright turquoise / coral alarm button.  
**Silhouette:** Tall clock body, twelve thin clock-hand arms, spring legs and bells.

## Personality and entrance
A theatrical clock who eats entire afternoons. He is offended when anyone is late. “IT'S NEVER TOO LATE… UNLESS I SAY SO!”

## Arena
A giant clock face made of moving platforms. Some floor tiles gently swing like pendulums. The number twelve is marked as the target zone.

## Moves and phases
1. **Minute-Hand Sweep:** A slow rotating hand casts an obvious shadow; players jump over it.
2. **Tick-Tock Freeze:** Tick-Tock freezes everything except the heroes for a short, predictable period, revealing a safe path.
3. **Backwards Hour (phase 2):** The arena rotates and arrows on the floor temporarily show the changed movement directions. Provide an option to disable reversed controls for younger players.

## Puzzle weakness
Wait until both hands point to twelve. Bounce from a spring tile onto his oversized red alarm button. The bell knocks loose a protective gear. Do this three times.

## Win moment and reward
His bells ring at the wrong time, then he laughs and becomes a polite little pocket watch. He gives **Time Pause**, a brief slow-motion ability usable on puzzle objects.

**Combat readability:** Use a countdown animation before any control change. Never surprise the player with an unannounced inversion.

**Suggested SFX:** Tick-tock, spring boing, giant cheerful alarm.
