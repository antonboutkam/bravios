# 01 — Blubberus the Slime King

**Realm:** The Slime Kingdom  
**Role:** First boss; teaches jumping and attack warnings.  
**Palette:** Electric violet / periwinkle / gold.  
**Silhouette:** Short, enormous, wobbly jelly blob with exactly seven mismatched eyes and a tilted crown.

## Personality and entrance
Blubberus is convinced that every puddle is his royal property. He rises from a giant jelly lake, sticks out his long tongue and announces: “BEHOLD! THE KING OF ALL THINGS SQUISHY!”

## Arena
A circular pool surrounded by soft green and blue islands. Three large jelly tiles gently wobble up and down; their shadows show where the player will land. Falling in slime gently bounces the character back to safety.

## Moves and phases
1. **Royal Belly Flop:** A wide purple ring appears; after a clear pause, Blubberus lands inside it and creates a bouncy shockwave.
2. **Mini-Me Mayhem:** He splits into several small jelly minions. Each has a different palette/eye pattern, following the global visual specification.
3. **Super Sneeze (phase 2):** His nose glows, his seven eyes cross and an enormous sneeze launches his crown into the air.

## Puzzle weakness
Bounce off a jelly tile and land on the crown while it is off his head. Repeat this three times. The crown is the target, not his face.

## Win moment and reward
Blubberus collapses into a mountain of giggling jelly and offers **Slime Bounce**, a power that lets heroes reach high tiles and survive long drops.

**Combat readability:** Violet circles signal a slam; a sparkling crown signals a safe hit window. Avoid rapid flashing.

**Suggested SFX:** Rubber boings, bubbling water, a comically oversized “ACHOO!”
