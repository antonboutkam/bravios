# 02 — Wafzilla the Terrible

**Realm:** The Candy Castle  
**Role:** Teaches avoiding sticky surfaces and using the arena against a boss.  
**Palette:** Golden toasted waffle / whipped white / strawberry red.  
**Silhouette:** Tall square waffle body, visible grid texture, syrup-cannon arms and butter feet.

## Personality and entrance
A furious walking waffle refuses to let anybody cross his breakfast table. “NO ONE ESCAPES THE MOST IMPORTANT MEAL OF THE DAY!”

## Arena
An oversized breakfast table with swinging spoon bridges, wobbly pancake platforms and a jam river. A giant butter dish moves along a predictable track.

## Moves and phases
1. **Syrup Splash:** Both arm cannons paint obvious amber danger strips onto the floor. Sticky strips slow movement but disappear after a few seconds.
2. **Flying Pancake:** He tosses spinning pancakes along straight, clearly marked paths. They can be hopped over.
3. **Breakfast Rush (phase 2):** He skates on his butter feet; the player can steer his path by standing near a safe lane.

## Puzzle weakness
Time a jump to hit the yellow butter block as Wafzilla rushes past. Butter lands under his feet and makes him slide into a soft cushion of whipped cream. Hit the exposed waffle emblem three times.

## Win moment and reward
He becomes a cheerful stack of tiny waffles and gifts **Syrup Swing**: a safe, sticky swing that attaches to special candy hooks.

**Combat readability:** Amber puddles mean slow; red-arrow tracks show moving projectiles. Sticky floor must be distinguishable from ordinary ground.

**Suggested SFX:** Toaster pop, syrup splat, silly slide whistle.
