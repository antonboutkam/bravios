# 03 — Sockstein

**Realm:** Lost Sock Island  
**Role:** Teaches escaping suction and activating an environmental device.  
**Palette:** Teal / denim blue / pumpkin orange, with multicolored sock patches.  
**Silhouette:** Eight long fabric arms, thin lumpy torso, stitched patches and googly eyes.

## Personality and entrance
Sockstein is an enormous living pile of socks lost behind washing machines. He is looking for the partner of every sock he owns. “I'M NOT STINKY. I'M VINTAGE!”

## Arena
An island shaped like a laundry basket, with rotating clothesline bridges, bouncy cushions and a friendly giant washing machine in the center. Small stepping tiles swing slightly when approached.

## Moves and phases
1. **Stinky Sock Toss:** Lobs cartoon stink clouds; marked green circles briefly make players cough and move slowly.
2. **Vacuum Vortex:** His backpack vacuum pulls loose socks and heroes toward a bright, visible funnel.
3. **Eight-Arm Whirl (phase 2):** His arms spin in a predictable pattern; duck behind laundry baskets or hop between safe tiles.

## Puzzle weakness
Turn the washing machine on by stepping on both large color-coded buttons (in co-op, one hero per button; in solo, use a short timer). Lure Sockstein into its harmless bubble vortex. After spinning, he gets dizzy and drops his protective sock helmet.

## Win moment and reward
All the socks pair themselves up and dance in a conga line. Sockstein awards **Sock Cyclone**, a gentle air swirl that moves loose objects and clears dust.

**Combat readability:** Suction direction is shown with arrows and floating socks; safe cover is marked by a contrasting outline.

**Suggested SFX:** Washing-machine hum, stretchy twangs, happy sock squeaks.
