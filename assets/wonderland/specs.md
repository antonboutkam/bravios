Bravios in Wonderland – World Design Instructions
1. World Concept
Create a surreal, colorful, and psychedelic fantasy world called Bravios in Wonderland.

This world should feel unpredictable, magical, and slightly absurd. Normal laws of physics do not always apply. Roads may change direction, castles may move, gravity can reverse, and ordinary objects can become enormous.

The visual style should be playful, vibrant, and suitable for children aged 5–8.

Use:
- Bright neon colors, rainbow gradients, and glowing objects.
- Giant mushrooms, floating islands, and twisted trees.
- Spiral patterns, optical illusions, and impossible architecture.
- Animated environments that constantly change and react to the player.
- Whimsical, funny, and surprising creatures.
The world consists of interconnected level nodes connected by paths. Players move between nodes, encounter branching routes, unlock new areas, and travel between islands.
2. World Areas
Area 1: The Mushroom Kingdom
Create a magical forest filled with enormous, colorful mushrooms.

Environment:
- Giant mushrooms with spiral patterns and glowing spots.
- Smiling flowers and trees with twisted trunks.
- Mushroom houses and bouncing platforms.
- An upside-down castle hanging beneath an enormous mushroom.
- Hidden tunnels connecting different parts of the forest.
Gameplay mechanics:
- Players can jump on mushrooms to reach otherwise inaccessible areas.
- Special mushrooms temporarily shrink or enlarge the player.
- Some mushrooms teleport players to different locations.
- Mushroom bridges appear and disappear.
- Certain paths become accessible only when the player changes size.
Area 2: The Chessboard Castle
Create a surreal castle surrounded by floating chessboards and impossible staircases.

Environment:
- Black-and-white checkerboard roads.
- Giant animated chess pieces.
- Floating platforms that continuously rearrange themselves.
- Staircases that appear to lead in impossible directions.
- A crooked castle that rotates or changes orientation.
Gameplay mechanics:
- Chessboard platforms move after certain player actions.
- Giant chess pieces act as moving obstacles or enemies.
- Gravity can temporarily reverse.
- Players must activate switches to rearrange pathways.
- Some doors lead to unexpected locations.
Area 3: The Rainbow Crystal Castle
Create a magnificent castle on a floating island surrounded by waterfalls, rainbows, and enormous glowing crystals.

This area serves as the final destination of the world.

Environment:
- A giant castle constructed from colorful crystals.
- Rainbow bridges connecting floating islands.
- Magical waterfalls flowing upward.
- Floating crystals that rotate and change color.
- A constantly shifting psychedelic sky.
Gameplay mechanics:
- Rainbow bridges appear when specific challenges are completed.
- Crystals can change the direction of gravity.
- Players must activate colored crystals in the correct sequence.
- Some platforms become solid only when their colors match.
- The final castle contains the world's main boss encounter.
Area 4: The Dream Sea
Create a magical sea separating the main island from the Rainbow Crystal Castle.

Environment:
- Deep blue and purple water filled with glowing spiral patterns.
- Floating islands and mysterious rock formations.
- Small docks with magical boats.
- Giant teacups floating on the water.
- Strange sea creatures and colorful whirlpools.
Gameplay mechanics:
- Players travel between islands using magical boats.
- Boats automatically follow predefined routes.
- Some boat routes must be unlocked.
- Magical whirlpools can transport boats to hidden locations.
- Secret islands contain bonus levels and collectibles.
3. Special World Mechanics
Mechanic 1: Reversed Controls
Certain levels or magical areas temporarily reverse player controls.

Instructions:
- Reverse horizontal movement when the player activates a special object or enters a designated area.
- Clearly indicate when controls are reversed.
- Restore normal controls after a short duration or when leaving the area.
- Use visual effects to communicate the change.
Mechanic 2: Size-Changing Mushrooms
Allow players to temporarily shrink or grow.

Instructions:
- Create two types of magical mushrooms: shrinking and growing.
- Small players can access narrow tunnels and hidden passages.
- Large players can move heavy objects or reach elevated platforms.
- Size transformations should be temporary.
- Ensure players cannot become permanently trapped.
Mechanic 3: Changing Pathways
Make certain overworld paths dynamically change.

Instructions:
- Create paths that appear, disappear, rotate, or reconnect.
- Trigger these changes after completing levels or activating switches.
- Allow branching routes that lead to different challenges.
- Show animated transitions whenever the map changes.
- Never leave the player without a valid route.
Mechanic 4: Gravity Manipulation
Allow gravity to behave differently in designated levels.

Instructions:
- Create areas where gravity pulls upward, sideways, or downward.
- Allow players to walk on walls and ceilings.
- Use special crystals or switches to change gravity.
- Provide visual cues showing the current gravity direction.
- Design puzzles requiring players to manipulate gravity.
Mechanic 5: The Lost Boat
Create a magical boat that occasionally travels to unexpected locations.

Instructions:
- Allow players to board boats at designated docks.
- Normally, boats should follow predictable routes.
- Under special conditions, a boat may travel to a secret island.
- Use magical effects to indicate unusual journeys.
- Secret destinations should reward players with collectibles or bonus challenges.
- Always provide a way to return to the main world.
Mechanic 6: The Moving Castle
Create a castle that changes its position on the overworld map.

Instructions:
- Move the castle to a different predefined location after specific levels are completed.
- Animate the castle moving, floating, or teleporting.
- Update connected paths when its position changes.
- Use its movement to reveal previously inaccessible areas.
- Ensure the castle remains reachable when required for progression.
4. Overworld Map Requirements
Create an interactive world map connecting all four areas.

Requirements:
1. Use clearly visible circular level nodes.
2. Connect nodes using winding, colorful pathways.
3. Include multiple branching routes.
4. Allow players to select different paths at intersections.
5. Include at least three major castles.
6. Connect separate islands using magical boats.
7. Unlock new paths and areas as players progress.
8. Include hidden bonus levels and secret routes.
9. Animate environmental objects such as mushrooms, clouds, crystals, and waterfalls.
10. Maintain a consistent visual style across the entire world.
5. Design Principles
- Surprise: Regularly introduce unexpected visual changes and unusual interactions.
- Exploration: Reward players for investigating alternative routes.
- Accessibility: Keep controls understandable for young children.
- Variety: Give each area its own visual identity and gameplay challenges.
- Progression: Gradually introduce new mechanics before combining them.
- Playfulness: Favor funny, imaginative interactions over frustrating difficulty.
Important: Although Wonderland should feel chaotic and unpredictable, its underlying gameplay must remain understandable. Players should always be able to recognize where they can move, which paths are available, and how to continue their adventure.








- Some platforms should connect two paths, allowing players to cross gaps when the platform reaches the correct position.
- Add a few magical platforms that swing in unusual patterns, such as circular or figure-eight movements.
- Make the swinging motion smooth, playful, and slightly exaggerated.
- Ensure players cannot accidentally fall off while traveling on the overworld map.
Special gameplay idea:

Create a swinging platform that connects three different paths. Depending on when the player activates it, the platform carries them to a different destination.

Visual style: Use colorful wooden platforms, glowing ropes, oversized flowers, and psychedelic particle effects. The platforms should fit naturally into Wonderland's surreal environment.
