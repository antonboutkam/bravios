# 05 — DJ Thunderfluff

**Realm:** Thunder Disco  
**Role:** A rhythm boss with an accessible non-rhythm mode.  
**Palette:** Bubblegum pink / electric cyan / charcoal sunglasses.  
**Silhouette:** Floating fluffy storm cloud with sunglasses, lightning hair and speaker mittens.

## Personality and entrance
DJ Thunderfluff thinks every thunderstorm should be a dance party. “WELCOME TO THE LOUDEST CLOUD IN WONDERLAND!”

## Arena
A floating disco dance floor with big square tiles. Some tiles sway gently like suspended dance platforms. Beat patterns appear as colored floor symbols before lightning lands.

## Moves and phases
1. **Lightning Beat:** Lightning strikes only the highlighted tiles, on a slow four-beat count.
2. **Bass Blast:** Speaker mittens push heroes sideways, but never into an unavoidable hazard.
3. **Dance-Off (phase 2):** A four-step sequence of left, right, jump and stomp repeats with gradually changing order.

## Puzzle weakness
Repeat the short dance pattern using footstep tiles. Three correct sequences overheat his sound system, which bursts into confetti rather than breaking dangerously.

## Win moment and reward
DJ Thunderfluff lowers his sunglasses, announces the heroes are better DJs and teaches **Beat Dash**, a musical burst of speed that crosses small gaps.

**Accessibility:** Use pictograms and tile pulses in addition to audio. Offer a “follow the lights” mode with no timing penalty and a toggle for reduced animation.

**Suggested SFX:** Playful drum loop, bass woof, thunder pop, applause.
