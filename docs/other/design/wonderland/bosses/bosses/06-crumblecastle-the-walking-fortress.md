# 06 — Crumblecastle the Walking Fortress

**Realm:** The Wandering Castle  
**Role:** Teaches reading terrain changes while the boss itself becomes the level.  
**Palette:** Moss green / sandstone beige / crimson flag.  
**Silhouette:** Asymmetric castle towers, drawbridge mouth, four enormous chicken legs.

## Personality and entrance
A grumpy castle is tired of visitors trampling its carpets. It stomps out of the forest and shouts: “TRESPASSERS WILL BE REDECORATED!”

## Arena
A checkered meadow of moving paths, wobbling flagstone platforms and two small bridges. The castle walks from one island to another, creating and removing walkable routes.

## Moves and phases
1. **Furniture Cannon:** Pillows, chairs and cushions fly along pre-marked arcs and land as temporary stepping stones.
2. **Chicken-Leg Stomp:** One leg lifts high, showing a large footprint shadow; after a pause it stomps.
3. **Drawbridge Chomp (phase 2):** The bridge opens as a silly mouth and gently pushes nearby heroes away.

## Puzzle weakness
Use the cushion stepping stones to climb up a lowered leg and reach the highest turret. Pull the bright crimson flag; it makes the castle sneeze and sit down. Repeat after it stands again.

## Win moment and reward
The castle folds its legs, becomes a cozy tiny playhouse and hands over **Castle Stomp**, a ground-pound that can activate large switches.

**Combat readability:** Shadow decals show stomps; bridge edges always have safety rail indicators when moving.

**Suggested SFX:** Chicken cluck, stone rumble, trumpet fanfare, squeaky door.
