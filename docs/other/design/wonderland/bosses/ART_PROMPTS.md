# Boss Concept Art Prompts (English)

Use one prompt per boss. Keep the shared art direction consistent while preserving each character's own silhouette and palette. These prompts are suitable for creating character concepts, **not** a finished animation sprite sheet.

## Shared art direction

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: isolated game boss concept art for *Bravios in Wonderland*, a surreal, colorful, child-friendly adventure. Premium whimsical 3D cartoon platformer character with a sculpted toy-like silhouette, soft materials, painterly detail and cheerful psychedelic Wonderland accents. Full body, front three-quarter view, centered with margins, clean light background or transparent cutout, readable at thumbnail size. A silly but imposing boss, suitable for ages 5–8. No scenery, no UI, no labels, no logos, no watermark. The character must be visibly unlike all other bosses in both silhouette and dominant palette.

## Individual subjects

1. **Blubberus the Slime King:** A gigantic squat translucent electric-violet jelly monster, exactly seven mismatched googly eyes, long curled pink tongue, tilted bright-gold crown and wobbling jelly arms. Periwinkle glints; very wide soft blob silhouette; no waffle, fabric or clock parts.
2. **Wafzilla the Terrible:** A towering golden-brown square living waffle with crisp grid texture, whipped-cream hair, giant berry-red strawberry eyes, two syrup cannons as arms and slippery butter feet. Warm toast-gold, white and strawberry red; rectangular stacked silhouette; no slime.
3. **Sockstein:** A gangly patchwork creature stitched from hundreds of mismatched socks, eight ridiculously long knitted sock arms, giant expressive eyes, a tiny toy vacuum carried on its back. Dominant teal and denim blue with pumpkin-orange accents, stitched seams, stripes and mismatched toes; no shiny metal body.
4. **Tick-Tock the Time Eater:** A giant antique-brass alarm clock monster with a mischievous face in its clock dial, twelve skinny clock-hand arms, tall spring legs, rattling bells and bright turquoise gear interiors. Warm brass, turquoise and small coral-red alarm button; thin angular mechanical silhouette.
5. **DJ Thunderfluff:** A floating bubblegum-pink thundercloud monster wearing massive dark sunglasses, with lightning-bolt hair, electric-cyan lightning arcs and oversized speaker mittens. Puffy round airborne cloud silhouette, rhythmic joyful expression; no crown or feet.
6. **Crumblecastle the Walking Fortress:** A living fairy-tale castle with four absurd chicken legs, wide expressive eyes in two towers, a drawbridge mouth, moss-covered sandstone walls, red fluttering flag and playful castle turrets. Tall architectural irregular silhouette, moss green and sandy stone, not metallic.
7. **Princess Mirrorphantom:** An elegant but goofy giant chameleon princess with three expressive faces, a long coiled tail and sharp faceted reflective mirror wings; silvery-lavender scales, cobalt-blue facets and a small crooked jeweled tiara. Long graceful angular silhouette; avoid glass fragments or violent motifs.
8. **Emperor Wobbleflap the Everything-Eater:** The grand final silly monster, huge midnight-indigo and deep-plum body with a glowing star-filled belly, three DIFFERENT heads, six mismatched arms and a flamboyant floating rainbow crown set with seven gemstones. Spectacular layered silhouette, colorful magical accents without replacing the dark indigo base. Playful rather than scary.

## Output advice

- Generate each character as a separate illustration for easy cropping and comparison.
- Preserve the palette, proportions and key identifying details across future poses.
- Verify limb counts, faces, and accessories manually; image-generation systems can drift.
- For game sprites, draw or generate animation frames separately after the concept designs are approved.
