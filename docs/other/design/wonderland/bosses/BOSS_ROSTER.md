# Boss Roster — Bravios in Wonderland

The eight bosses each rule a different surreal region. Their art direction, main color palettes, silhouettes, combat rhythms and puzzle weaknesses must be recognizably different.

| # | Boss | Realm | Visual signature | Unique mechanic | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Blubberus the Slime King | The Slime Kingdom | Violet jelly, seven eyes, gold crown; wide blob | Split, bounce and sneeze | Slime Bounce |
| 2 | Wafzilla the Terrible | The Candy Castle | Golden waffle, red berries, white cream; tall square | Sticky syrup and sliding butter | Syrup Swing |
| 3 | Sockstein | Lost Sock Island | Teal patchwork socks with orange accents; eight fabric arms | Vacuum suction and washing-machine spin | Sock Cyclone |
| 4 | Tick-Tock the Time Eater | The Backwards Clockwork | Brass and turquoise clockwork; spindly spring legs | Reversed movement and stopped time | Time Pause |
| 5 | DJ Thunderfluff | Thunder Disco | Bubblegum-pink storm cloud, electric-blue lightning; floating round cloud | Rhythm and lightning patterns | Beat Dash |
| 6 | Crumblecastle the Walking Fortress | The Wandering Castle | Moss-green stone and sandy towers; four chicken legs | Moving arena and falling furniture | Castle Stomp |
| 7 | Princess Mirrorphantom | The Mirror Palace | Silvery lavender chameleon with cobalt mirror wings; long angular tail | Reflections, clones and invisibility | Mirror Twin |
| 8 | Emperor Wobbleflap the Everything-Eater | The Edge of Wonderland | Midnight-indigo body, luminous rainbow crown; three heads and six arms | Remix of all seven earlier bosses | Wonderland Restored |

## Difficulty and placement

Bosses 1–3 introduce one clear idea each. Bosses 4–6 combine movement with arena changes. Boss 7 emphasizes observation. Boss 8 is a collaborative finale that tests all learned mechanics.

Each fight begins with a safe demonstration of its special attack. Let the player win by recognizing a pattern rather than needing fast reflexes. Defeated bosses become friendly characters or harmless forms.

For every boss, keep a unique color palette **and** silhouette. The strict visual-differentiation requirements for bosses and ordinary enemies are specified in `ENEMY_VISUAL_SPEC.md`.
